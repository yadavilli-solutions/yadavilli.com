## Extract the Intelligence Trapped in Your Systems

**DeepDive** reverse-engineers your codebase and documentation to construct rich, queryable knowledge graphs. It transforms the implicit knowledge embedded in legacy systems into explicit, structured intelligence that can power AI-driven decision-making.

### What DeepDive Does

- **Automated Code Analysis**: Parse and analyze codebases across languages and frameworks. DeepDive understands control flow, data dependencies, business rules, and integration patterns.
- **Documentation Extraction**: Ingest technical documentation, runbooks, wikis, and operational guides, extracting procedures into structured form.
- **Knowledge Graph Construction**: Build queryable knowledge graphs mapping relationships between systems, processes, data flows, and business logic across your technology estate.
- **Business Logic Mapping**: Identify and extract the business rules embedded in legacy code, the institutional knowledge that's critical for modernization and often undocumented.

### How It Fits the Pipeline

DeepDive represents the Decode phase of our Discover · Calibrate · Decode · Activate framework. It takes the technology architecture assessment from [BenchMark](/products/benchmark/) to **extract the knowledge needed to power [KnowHow](/products/knowhow/) runbook generation**.

### Licensing

DeepDive is licensed per engagement and includes a dedicated full-stack development engineer and two operations engineers to **maintain and configure the knowledge graph**.
