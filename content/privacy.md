Last updated: November 5, 2026

Yadavilli Solutions ("we", "us") runs this website. This policy covers what we collect when you visit it or contact us. The short version: we run no analytics, set no cookies, and load nothing from third parties.

## What we collect

**Server logs.** Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, receives every request to the site. That includes your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for, and the time. Cloudflare uses this to deliver pages, block abuse, and diagnose faults. We can see request logs in our Cloudflare account for troubleshooting.

**Email you send us.** If you write to hello@yadavilli.com, we receive your address, your message, and anything you attach. We use it to reply and to deal with the matter you raised.

**Theme preference.** If you switch the site between light and dark, your browser stores that choice in its own local storage. It never leaves your device, and we cannot read it.

## What we do not collect

- No analytics or behavior tracking.
- No cookies of any kind.
- No fonts, scripts, or embeds loaded from other companies. Fonts are served from this site.
- No contact form. Email is the only way to send us data through this site.

## Why we process it

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis, where GDPR or UK GDPR applies |
|---|---|---|
| Server logs | Deliver the site, security, diagnostics | Legitimate interests |
| Email correspondence | Answer your inquiry | Legitimate interests, or contract |

## Who else sees it

- **Cloudflare** hosts the site and handles request data as our processor. Its own privacy terms apply to that processing.
- **Our email provider** stores correspondence.
- **Advisers and authorities**, only when the law requires it or we need professional advice.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

## Links to other sites

Where this site links elsewhere, those sites set their own cookies and keep their own records once you leave this one. Read their policies before you sign in or post.

## How long we keep it

Correspondence stays as long as the matter or a legal duty requires. Request logs follow Cloudflare's retention settings for our account.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict its use, or hand you a copy. You can also object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email hello@yadavilli.com. We reply within 30 days. If you think we handled your data badly, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

This site is written for business readers. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

## Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
