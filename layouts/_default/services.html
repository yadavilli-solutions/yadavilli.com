Operationalization of AI POCs

What this includes

Agentic Process Automation

Rules-based RPA breaks the moment a screen, form, or document layout changes, and the maintenance bill outgrows the savings it was bought for. We replace brittle bot estates with agents that work from process intent, call systems through APIs, and escalate only the exceptions that need a person.

What this includes
  • Bot estate assessment and migration sequencing
  • Agent design for exception-heavy processes
  • API-first integration in place of screen scraping
  • Human approval gates and decision audit trails
  • Straight-through processing rate as the success metric

AI Platform Operations

The layer that decides whether AI survives contact with production: safety, spend, and observability. Most AI programs discover they need all three the week after launch. We build them once, instrument them, and hand over the dashboards.

What this includes
  • Guardrails: input validation, output filtering, hallucination and toxicity checks
  • Compliance enforcement and decision audit trails
  • Model performance monitoring and drift detection
  • GPU right-sizing and batch vs. real-time cost trade-offs
  • Cost attribution that ties AI spend to business outcomes
  • Automated remediation pipelines
